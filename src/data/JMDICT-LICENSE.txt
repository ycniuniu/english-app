The Japanese vocabulary bundled in src/data/jlpt-words.js is a derived
work built for this app.

It uses:

- JMdict, by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group.
  https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html
  Licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- OpenJLPT N5–N1 vocabulary lists, which compile Jonathan Waller's JLPT
  lists with JMdict readings and parts of speech.
  https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT
  Licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Short Chinese glosses aligned to those English senses, with help from
  ECDICT (MIT) by skywind3000 / Linwei.

Example sentences and collocations in the word cards are original
learner lines written for this app.

The vocabulary data in src/data/jlpt-words.js is released under
CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

JLPT levels are community lists. The Japan Foundation does not publish
an official N5–N1 word list. After the N5–N1 entries, high-frequency
JMdict headwords fill the list out to about twelve thousand.
